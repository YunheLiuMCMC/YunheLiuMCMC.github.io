Yunhe Liu

My name is Yunhe Liu; welcome to my website! I am a PhD student in the Department of Statistics and Data Sciences at the University of Texas at Austin, under the direction of Professor Yang Ni. I completed my master's in Biostatistics at Yale University, under the direction of Professor Haitao Pan, Professor Wei Wei, and Professor Yize Zhao. I received my B.S. from Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University, under the direction of Professor Youzhou Zhou and Professor Xiaojun Zhu.

Research

My past work has spanned survival analysis, clinical trial design, neuroimaging, and psychometrics. My current focus is on how Bayesian Statistics and Causal Inference can better answer the scientific questions that arise from biomedical data, such as spatial omics. My research philosophy is to start from real-world problems and, in turn, develop practical, transparent, and robust tools that the research community can readily use.
